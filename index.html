About us

Twelve years of raising chickens the patient way

CluckCart Poultry started with twelve hens and one borrowed shed. Today we keep a small, carefully managed flock of free-range, heritage and commercial layer breeds on a family farm in Batangas. We would rather sell you four healthy birds than forty weak ones.

Every chicken in our catalogue is raised on our own farm, fed with locally milled feed and checked by hand twice a day. That is why our stock numbers are honest and our customers come back season after season.

  • Raised on our own farm - no middlemen, no resellers
  • Vaccinated and health-checked before listing
  • Clear weight and age for every single bird
  • Friendly after-sales support for new flock owners
