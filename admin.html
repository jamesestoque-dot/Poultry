How this demo works

  • Product edits are saved in this browser with localStorage and survive a refresh.
  • Availability is calculated automatically from the stock quantity.
  • The age of a chicken decides its category: 4 months, 5 months, or 6 months and older.
  • Sample orders placed on the checkout page appear under "Order Management".
A production system needs a backend, secure authentication and a real database before it can be used for a live business.
